# Emulator credits

This player bundles TSRun by Josef Jelinek at commit
`41dbe3e41c692a1806f2cb900c5a0faa1549ae77`, including its stock TS2068 system
ROMs. The runtime is copied without modifications into the Pages build artifact.
Its [original README](tsrun/README.md) and [upstream source](https://github.com/josef-jelinek/TSRun)
retain their authorship and rights. File checksums are in `build-manifest.json`.

TSWriter and its font credits are documented in the repository's
[third-party notices](https://github.com/jon0x0/TSWriter/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
