The live sample uses web-font copies of the matching desktop package. All styles supplied for its six font families are available; the browser downloads the faces needed by the displayed text.

These are outline companions to the TSWriter faces. They do not promise identical native line breaks. SirClive has one face; a browser may synthesize bold or italic.
